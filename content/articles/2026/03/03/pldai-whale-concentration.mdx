Smart money portfolios are showing extreme concentration with PLDAI commanding a staggering $3.77 billion position across just two wallets, representing nearly 70% of all tracked smart money holdings. The 6.2-year-old yield-bearing token's dominance in sophisticated investor portfolios highlights the continued appetite for yield-generating assets, even as the position size creates significant concentration risk. With PLDAI's market cap sitting at just $546 million, the smart money position appears to represent a significant portion of the token's total supply, suggesting these whales either have early investor status or are participating in specialized yield programs unavailable to retail traders.
