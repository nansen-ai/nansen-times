The memecoin landscape on Solana is painting a picture of selective smart money positioning, with established player NEET attracting $2,729 in weekly inflows while GameStop-themed GME faces $2,519 in daily outflows. NEET, a 310-day-old token with a robust $24.4 million market cap, continues to draw sophisticated investors who appear to favor its longevity over newer meme plays. Conversely, the 766-day-old GME token, despite its larger market presence, is experiencing smart money flight, suggesting traders may be rotating out of stock-themed memecoins in favor of more original concepts.
