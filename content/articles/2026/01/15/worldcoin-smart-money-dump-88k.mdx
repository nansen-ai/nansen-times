Worldcoin's WLD token is facing sustained selling pressure from smart money, with three tracked traders dumping a combined $88,286 over the past seven days. The coordinated exit from the $1.54 billion project suggests growing skepticism among sophisticated investors about the AI-focused token's near-term prospects.

The selling intensity has accelerated dramatically, with $15,519 worth of WLD liquidated in just the past 24 hours. Over the monthly timeframe, these same traders have shed a staggering $181,694 worth of positions, representing one of the largest sustained outflows in the smart money dataset.

Despite WLD's massive market capitalization and backing from prominent Silicon Valley figures, the consistent distribution pattern indicates that even well-informed traders are losing conviction. The token's 913-day trading history hasn't been enough to maintain smart money confidence, raising questions about whether institutional sentiment is shifting away from AI-themed crypto projects.
