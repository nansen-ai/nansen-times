The GameStop-inspired meme token GME is hemorrhaging smart money as a whale trader executed a brutal $25,801 selloff in the past 24 hours, according to exclusive Nansen data. The massive outflow represents the largest single-day exodus among all tracked positions, signaling potential trouble for the $15.8 million market cap token.

The selling pressure extends beyond yesterday's dump, with the same trader offloading $29,315 worth of GME over the past month. This sustained distribution pattern suggests the whale has completely lost faith in the token's prospects, despite GME's nearly two-year track record in the market.

The timing of this exit is particularly noteworthy as it comes amid broader meme coin volatility. With only one major holder driving these outflows, the token faces the risk of further capitulation if other smart money addresses follow suit. Market observers are now watching closely for any signs of contagion spreading to other meme positions in whale portfolios.
