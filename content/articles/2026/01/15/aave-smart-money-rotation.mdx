Aave, the $2.59 billion DeFi lending giant, is experiencing notable smart money outflows with four traders selling $10,160 worth of AAVE tokens in the past day. The selling represents part of a larger trend, with $66,249 worth of AAVE distributed by smart money over the past month. Despite the protocol's strong fundamentals and nearly 5-year track record, sophisticated traders appear to be rotating out of the position, potentially seeking higher-yield opportunities in the current market environment.
