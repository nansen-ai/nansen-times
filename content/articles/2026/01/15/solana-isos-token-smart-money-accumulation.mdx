A barely week-old Solana memecoin called ISOS is catching smart money attention despite its microscopic $84,520 market cap. Two sophisticated traders have steadily accumulated $3,422 worth of the Bags.fm-listed token over the past month, with $1,705 added in the last 24 hours alone.

The accumulation pattern shows remarkable conviction for such a new asset, with consistent buying occurring over multiple timeframes. The token's 7-day age makes it one of the youngest assets to attract meaningful smart money flows in recent data, suggesting these traders may have identified early-stage potential in the Bags.fm ecosystem.

While the absolute dollar amounts remain small, the percentage impact on ISOS's tiny market cap could be substantial. Smart money's willingness to take positions in such nascent projects often signals either insider knowledge or sophisticated risk-reward calculations that retail investors may be missing.
