The selloff is particularly notable given A0T's 323-day track record and $3.82 million market cap, suggesting this was a long-held position rather than a quick trade gone wrong. The exit marks rare smart money selling activity on Base chain, where elite traders typically maintain smaller experimental positions. With AI memes facing broader market skepticism, the A0T dump may signal smart money's declining confidence in the intersection of artificial intelligence and memecoin narratives across alternative chains.
