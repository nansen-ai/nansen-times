Smart money wallets are making aggressive bets on brand-new Solana memecoins, accumulating $24,173 across four freshly minted tokens in the past 24 hours. The buying spree focuses heavily on Bags.fm launches, with KMIKEYM ($5,864), MEMEABLE ($3,954), and a MAYOR variant ($3,214) all seeing immediate elite trader interest despite being less than 48 hours old.

The most intriguing development involves two separate MAYOR tokens launching simultaneously, with smart money accumulating both versions. The larger MAYOR deployment attracted $11,142 in smart money flows and achieved a $711,837 market cap, while its competitor secured $3,214 in elite trader backing with a $148,608 valuation. This dual-token scenario suggests either coordinated launches or sophisticated traders hedging their bets across multiple iterations.

Bags.fm continues to dominate new memecoin launches on Solana, with three of the four tracked tokens originating from the platform. The speed of smart money deployment—most purchases occurring within hours of token creation—indicates these wallets are either running automated systems or have advance intelligence on promising new launches. With trader counts remaining extremely low (1-2 wallets per token), these appear to be concentrated bets rather than broad community adoption plays.
