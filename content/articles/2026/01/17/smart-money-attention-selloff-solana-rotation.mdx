Smart money wallets tracked by Nansen have orchestrated a dramatic exit from ATTENTION token, dumping $10,742 worth in 24 hours while simultaneously rotating into fresh Solana memecoins. The coordinated selloff represents the largest outflow among tracked tokens, signaling a potential end to ATTENTION's brief momentum.

Meanwhile, the same smart money cohort has been aggressively accumulating newer Bags.fm launches, with OMEGA leading inflows at $6,284 over 24 hours despite launching just today. The pattern suggests sophisticated traders are frontrunning retail rotation cycles, identifying optimal exit and entry points across the memecoin ecosystem.

The timing appears calculated: ATTENTION's $150K market cap provided sufficient liquidity for the exit, while the targeted accumulation of sub-$500K cap tokens like OMEGA, GSD, and MEM indicates a preference for earlier-stage opportunities. This rotation strategy has become a hallmark of professional memecoin trading, where timing and size selection prove critical for maximizing returns.

With trader counts remaining low across both inflow and outflow positions, this appears to be the work of a small number of highly capitalized wallets executing coordinated strategies rather than broader market sentiment shifts.
