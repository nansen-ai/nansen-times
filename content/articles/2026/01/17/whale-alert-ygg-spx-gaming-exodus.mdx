Established crypto projects are facing coordinated selling pressure from smart money wallets, with Yield Guild Games (YGG) experiencing $3,802 in 24-hour outflows from three tracked wallets. The gaming-focused DAO token, despite its $53M market cap and 1,636-day track record, appears to be falling out of favor among sophisticated traders.

Simultaneously, SPX has endured more severe punishment with $109,920 in weekly outflows from four smart money addresses. The Ethereum-based memecoin's $519M market cap hasn't provided immunity against the systematic selling, suggesting fundamental shifts in institutional appetite for older memecoin projects.

The multi-chain nature of these exits indicates broader portfolio rebalancing rather than chain-specific concerns. While YGG's gaming sector exposure may reflect skepticism about GameFi recovery, the SPX selling suggests even large-cap memecoins aren't immune to profit-taking when smart money identifies better opportunities elsewhere.
