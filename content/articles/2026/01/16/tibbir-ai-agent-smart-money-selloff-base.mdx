A coordinated smart money exodus from TIBBIR is raising red flags across the AI agent token sector, with three sophisticated wallets dumping $9,687 worth of the Base-chain token in the past 24 hours. The selloff extends a concerning 7-day pattern that has seen $14,795 in outflows, despite the token maintaining a substantial $183 million market cap and nearly year-long track record.

TIBBIR's positioning at the intersection of memecoins and AI agents made it a darling of the narrative-driven crypto cycles, but smart money appears to be rotating out ahead of potential headwinds. The timing is particularly notable given the broader AI agent token boom, suggesting these whale wallets may have inside information or technical analysis pointing to fundamental weaknesses in the project's execution.

The multi-chain nature of the selloff - with similar patterns emerging across Solana memecoins like TROLL and BITTY - suggests this could be part of a broader smart money rotation away from narrative-heavy tokens toward more fundamental plays. Retail traders holding AI agent tokens should monitor these whale movements closely as institutional selling often precedes broader market corrections in speculative sectors.
