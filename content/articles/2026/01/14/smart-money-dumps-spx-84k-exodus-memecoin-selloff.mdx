Smart money is fleeing SPX, the Ethereum memecoin, in what appears to be a coordinated exit strategy. Four sophisticated wallets collectively dumped $84,185 worth of SPX tokens over the past 24 hours, representing the token's largest outflow event tracked by onchain intelligence.

The selling pressure intensified over the past week, with net outflows reaching $106,269 as smart money wallets reduced their SPX exposure. Despite the token's substantial $572 million market cap, the sustained selling from proven profitable traders suggests underlying concerns about the memecoin's trajectory.

The exodus extends beyond recent activity, with 30-day outflows totaling nearly $185,000. This represents a significant shift in sentiment among the wallet cohort that has historically demonstrated superior alpha generation. Market observers are watching for potential contagion effects across other high-cap memecoins as smart money appears to be rotating out of speculative positions.
