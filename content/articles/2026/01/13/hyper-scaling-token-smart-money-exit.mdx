The scaling and connectivity sector is facing headwinds as smart money continues its retreat from HYPER, with one sophisticated trader offloading $118,694 worth of tokens in the past 24 hours. This latest selloff brings the total smart money outflow to $221K over the past month, suggesting sustained bearish sentiment toward the $31 million market cap project.

HYPER's consistent outflows from smart money wallets paint a concerning picture for the scaling token, especially as the broader infrastructure narrative has been gaining traction elsewhere in the market. The sustained selling pressure over multiple weeks indicates this isn't merely profit-taking but potentially reflects fundamental concerns about the project's competitive position.

With scaling solutions becoming increasingly crowded and competitive, smart money appears to be rotating toward more promising opportunities in the space. The $31 million market cap provides some downside buffer, but continued smart money exodus could signal broader institutional skepticism about HYPER's long-term viability in the scaling wars.
