Smart money is fleeing ATH (Aethir) as five whale wallets coordinated a massive $278,866 selloff in the past 24 hours, marking the largest outflow among tracked tokens. The AI infrastructure token, which operates in the artificial intelligence, staking, and DePIN sectors, has seen consistent selling pressure with $492K in outflows over the past month.

The sudden exodus from ATH comes despite the token maintaining a robust $176 million market cap, suggesting these sophisticated traders may be rotating into other opportunities or responding to fundamental concerns about the project's future. The coordinated nature of the selling, involving five separate smart money wallets, indicates this wasn't random profit-taking but a deliberate strategic exit.

This selloff represents one of the most significant smart money movements in the AI token space this week, with traders who have historically demonstrated superior market timing choosing to abandon their positions. The timing coincides with broader uncertainty in the AI token sector, where investors are becoming increasingly selective about which projects warrant continued investment.

Market participants should monitor whether this smart money exodus triggers broader selling pressure or if contrarian investors view the dip as a buying opportunity in the still-nascent AI infrastructure space.
