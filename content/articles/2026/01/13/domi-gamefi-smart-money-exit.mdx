The GameFi sector continues to struggle for institutional adoption as DOMI, a 4-year veteran in the gaming, metaverse, and NFT space, faces smart money redemptions of $9,030 in 24 hours. This exodus reflects broader skepticism about older GameFi projects' ability to compete in an evolving landscape.
